# Self-closure does not yet supply continuation reproduction

`lean4/Hypermath/TransportCountermodel.lean` proves that the 38 currently
declared logical clauses do not entail the reproduction needed for the
[conditional path-transport construction](PATH_TRANSPORT.md), even after
adding the current self-derivation proposition and its stronger two-step
cycle certificate. Both related starting forms in the counterexample are
generated by finite primitive forming from ground.

This is a result about the present formalization. The model's relation named
`Simulation` fails the stronger source description of mutual continuation
reproduction. That mismatch identifies operational content the declared
clauses have not captured; it does not refute the author's intended concept.

## The complete model and its obstruction

Forms are six distinct values. Primitive forming `f2f` and the labels defining
simulation are:

| Form | Next form | Simulation class | Position from ground |
|---|---|---|---|
| `a0` | `a1` | 0 | 0 |
| `a1` | `a2` | 1 | 1 |
| `a2` | `a3` | 2 | 2 |
| `a3` | `a4` | 1 | 3 |
| `a4` | `a3` | 1 | 4 |
| `limit` | `limit` | 3 | Not reached by finite forming from ground |

Class labels and finite positions are dimensionless natural numbers. The
labels are not ordinals or arithmetic truth ranks. Ground is `a0`, and the
deriver is `a3`. Simulation means equality of the displayed class labels;
`simulation_is_equivalence` proves reflexivity, symmetry, and transitivity.
Similarity (`~~`) and congruence (`=~`) are universal in this model.
Simulation (`==`) remains a separate, narrower relation.

Every forming edge is admitted by `DStep`, because its congruence guard is
true. The deriver's actual path `a3 -> a4 -> a3` preserves simulation at both
steps and closes. `cycleWitness` constructs the same three fields as the
native `DriverCycleWitness`: a simulation path, its actual length of two,
and endpoint simulation closure. `self_derivation_holds` separately proves
all four conjuncts of the current `selfDerivation` proposition.

Nevertheless, `a1` and `a3` have the same simulation label, while the witnessed
step `a1 -> a2` has **no finite reproduction from `a3`** whose endpoint
simulates `a2`. Every finite path from `a3` stays in `{a3, a4}` and hence in
class 1; `a2` belongs to class 2. The theorem `path_stays_in_cycle` proves the
invariant by induction over the actual path. `no_finite_reproduction` includes
all finite lengths, including zero. It is not inferred from the bounded probe.

Thus allowing several forming steps instead of one does not, by itself,
repair this particular declared model. Either the relatedness evidence or
the admissible continuation semantics must impose a stronger constraint.
No claim is made here about transfinite reproduction or different next-layer
carriers and operations.

## Why the geometry must determine observations

A simple design check rules out an inadequate replacement for simulation.
Suppose the only transitions are `x -> f(x)` for a total unary function `f`,
and no observations or guards are required to agree. Relate every pair of
states. For any step from `x`, the step from any `y` reaches `f(y)`, and the
two successors are again related. The same argument works in reverse.
Thus this universal relation satisfies that bare mutual step-matching rule.

This argument concerns unguarded, unobserved transitions, not the native
guarded `DStep`, and is not an assertion that native simulation is universal.
It shows why simply adding a generic step-matching definition would not
recover the intended distinctions. Verification geometry must supply the
observations or admissibility conditions that exclude inappropriate matches,
as well as evidence for reproducing the permitted paths. No particular
observation semantics is selected by this note.

## Author clarification: a cycle through the kernel roots

On September 12, the author specified: "A cycle in the inner self-derivation
core/kernel with pathway through the ground or self-derivation roots."
This identifies the kernel/root pathway as part of the intended verification
geometry. It does not, by itself, specify whether the connection is directed
both ways, how a relational return is traversed, or whether routing steps are
observationally silent. The following is a proposed concrete interpretation.

For a chosen root `r`, retain actual checked paths `r ->* x` and `x ->* r`
for each participating form `x`. Here `->*` means a finite path of justified
edges, including an empty path; a nontrivial cycle additionally requires
positive length. Given forms `x` and `y` with these witnesses, and an original
continuation `p : x ->* z`, construct:

`y ->* r ->* x ->* z`.

The first two segments are the supplied inward route from `y` and outward
route to `x`. The final segment is exactly `p`, with the same endpoint `z`.
This computes a replay from witnessed routes; it does not assume an arbitrary
transport operation. Swapping `x` and `y` gives the converse construction.
The additional routing is retained explicitly. For example, in the cycle
`r -> a -> b -> r`, the continuation `a -> b` can be replayed from `b` as
`b -> r -> a -> b`.

The local draft `build/RootedContinuationDraft.lean` checks six theorem
dependency reports. It proves exact retention of the continuation's edges
as a suffix, the added route length, compatibility with subsequent path
composition, and exact recovery of the grouped used routes and continuation
through any finite number of existing representation lifts. Three reports
use no axioms; three use only Lean's propositional extensionality. A concrete
three-vertex example also passed after four lifts. The proof was checked with
`lake env lean ../build/RootedContinuationDraft.lean` from `lean4`, with a
60-second process bound. Its SHA-256 digest is `ef6d4d1d29a14e80117d5c7fb734348a7eecca5ed23e3e73eaeb7d5327635377`.

The draft also proves that the countermodel's pair `a1`, `a3` cannot both
carry such two-way routes through any common root. A route from `a3` to `a1`
would contradict the already proved cycle invariant. Mere generation from
ground was insufficient; this candidate requires actual return and outward
paths for each participant. Naming `a3` as a root alone does not supply them.

This is a checked finite candidate, outside the production native audit and
package. It does not construct native kernel roots or their paths, identify
the candidate with native simulation `==`, or prove that additional routing
is invisible to every continuation. The lifted surface retains the routes
used for this replay; packaging both complete input cycle certificates as
reusable native forms remains further work. A literal return to ground is
not inferred from a similarity statement. The author's broader root pathway
may require a different transition or relational-return representation.

## Consequence for the next-layer bridge

The preservation theorems remain usable once an appropriate reproduction
operation is supplied. The next native step is to derive evidence of mutual
continuation reproduction from the intended verification geometry, with
an explicit observation relation and a rule for corresponding steps or
subpaths. It must exclude the pair `a1`, `a3` in this model as mutually
reproducing, or supply genuinely different admissible continuation structure.

The choice between one-for-one reproduction, finite subpath reproduction,
or a richer ranked rule remains open. The model adds no new native axiom,
changes no source relation, and establishes no arithmetic completeness result.
